Paste the receipt the picker gave you, as text or as JSON. This page works out the draw again from the names and the seed in it and tells you whether it matches. Nothing is uploaded: your browser does the arithmetic.

The commitment covers the names and the seed, and so the finishing order. The title, the time and the number of winners are labels on a draw and are not covered. RANDOMNESS.md says what that does and does not prove.
